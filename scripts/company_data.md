        ZED'S, owned by Mr. Francis Batangas, has been revolutionizing the hardware industry
since its inception in August 2020. What began as an online store quickly gained
popularity among DIY enthusiasts, professionals, and homeowners seeking reliable, high-
quality tools and equipment. With the overwhelming support from our valued customers,
we expanded our operations and proudly opened our physical store on April 25, 2021.
          We take pride in offering an extensive range of tools and equipment to meet diverse
needs. From power tools to hand tools, gardening equipment to safety gear—we have it

performance, and customer satisfaction. Whether it’s a small home improvement job or a
large-scale construction project, we have the perfect tools to get the job done.

standards. With ZED'S, you can expect durable tools, excellent performance, and
competitive prices.
